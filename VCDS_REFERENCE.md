## Troubleshooting Tips

### 1. Car Won't Start
**Root Cause Options:**
- Battery jump vs starter fault

**Diagnosis:**
- Check battery voltage with VCDS
- Run starter test
- Verify alternator output

**Quick Fix:** Jump start, battery registration
**Full Diagnosis:** Electrical system analysis
**Prevention:** Maintain battery SoH above 80%
**Cost Estimate:** €50-500 (battery: €80-150, starter: €350-500)

### 2. Check Engine Light (No Performance Issues)
**Root Cause:** False alarm diagnosis
**Quick Fix:** Clear DTC and monitor
**Full Diagnosis:** Fuel quality, vacuum leaks, timing chain issues
**Prevention:** Use premium fuel, regular maintenance
**Cost Estimate:** €0-300

### 3. Rough Idle
**Causes:** Fuel quality, vacuum leaks, timing chain
**Diagnosis:** Fuel injector cleaning test, vacuum leak detection
**Quick Fix:** Fuel system cleaning activation
**Prevention:** Quality fuel, regular service
**Cost Estimate:** €100-400

### 4. Spongy Brake Pedal
**Cause:** Air in ABS system
**Action:** MANDATORY - ABS bleeding via VCDS
**Diagnosis:** ABS self-test and buildup test
**Prevention:** Brake fluid inspection every 2 years
**Cost Estimate:** €150-300

### 5. TPMS Light After Rotation
**Cause:** Recalibration needed
**Quick Fix:** TPMS recalibration procedure
**Diagnosis:** Wheel speed sensor check
**Prevention:** Recalibrate after every rotation
**Cost Estimate:** €0 (DIY with VCDS)

### 6. Battery Dying After Replacement
**Cause:** Battery registration critical
**Action:** New battery registration MANDATORY
**Diagnosis:** Battery voltage trending, SoH analysis
**Prevention:** Always register new batteries
**Cost Estimate:** €0 (procedure only)

### 7. ESP Light + Vehicle Pulling
**Cause:** SAS calibration required
**Action:** Steering Angle Sensor calibration
**Diagnosis:** SAS offset adjustment, wheel speed verification
**Prevention:** Recalibrate after suspension work
**Cost Estimate:** €150-300

### 8. Transmission Slipping After Service
**Cause:** Adaptation reset required
**Quick Fix:** Transmission adaptation reset
**Diagnosis:** Fluid quality forensic read
**Prevention:** Allow adaptation time after service
**Cost Estimate:** €0-100

### 9. ACC Not Working / Collision
**Cause:** 12-step realignment procedure
**Action:** Radar/camera calibration
**Diagnosis:** Misalignment detection
**Prevention:** Check after any front-end work
**Cost Estimate:** €500-1000

### 10. Door Locks Don't Work
**Cause:** Solenoid vs BCM diagnosis
**Diagnosis:** Component voltage testing
**Quick Fix:** Door module reset
**Prevention:** Keep door seals maintained
**Cost Estimate:** €200-400

### 11. Steering Too Heavy
**Cause:** Power steering fluid vs EPS motor
**Diagnosis:** EPS motor current analysis
**Quick Fix:** Fluid top-up and bleed
**Prevention:** Annual power steering inspection
**Cost Estimate:** €100-600

### 12. Airbag Light
**Cause:** Clockspring failure (safety critical)
**Diagnosis:** Resistance verification
**Prevention:** Handle steering wheel carefully
**Cost Estimate:** €400-800

### 13. Windows Won't Move
**Cause:** Motor vs mechanical regulator
**Diagnosis:** Motor voltage and mechanical inspection
**Quick Fix:** Window reset procedure
**Prevention:** Keep window tracks clean
**Cost Estimate:** €200-400

### 14. Transmission Won't Engage
**Cause:** Fluid level, quality, or clutch wear
**Diagnosis:** Fluid forensic analysis
**Prevention:** Fluid quality monitoring
**Cost Estimate:** €200-2000

### 15. Fuel Smell
**Cause:** Line/hose leaks (safety critical)
**Action:** STOP DRIVING immediately
**Diagnosis:** Pressure test, injector test
**Cost Estimate:** €200-600

### 16. Limp Mode + Power Loss
**Cause:** Critical fault resolution
**Diagnosis:** DTC analysis and component testing
**Prevention:** Address issues early
**Cost Estimate:** €300-1500

### 17. No Key Fob Signal
**Cause:** Battery vs fob damage
**Diagnosis:** Battery voltage check, receiver sensitivity tuning
**Prevention:** Replace batteries proactively
**Cost Estimate:** €20-200

### 18. Brake Fluid Leak
**Cause:** System failure (stop driving)
**Action:** MANDATORY - Do not drive
**Diagnosis:** System pressure test
**Cost Estimate:** €500-1200

### 19. Oil Pressure Light
**Cause:** Low oil (engine seizure risk)
**Action:** Check oil level immediately
**Diagnosis:** Pressure sensor verification
**Prevention:** Regular oil level checks
**Cost Estimate:** €100-3000 (severe damage possible)

### 20. Radio / Infotainment Frozen
**Cause:** Reboot vs software update
**Diagnosis:** Software update check & apply
**Quick Fix:** Hard reboot procedure
**Prevention:** Keep software current
**Cost Estimate:** €0-300
